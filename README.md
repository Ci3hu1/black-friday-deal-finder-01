Price Ledger is a Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal finder: 34 real deals across Amazon, Best Buy and Walmart, each checked on 27 Nov 2026 and listed with its store link, sale price, usual price, discount, an honest verdict (NEW LOW / STRONG / ROUTINE) and the deals source the price was read in. Search and filters by category, store, discount and verdict all work, and the same product is compared across stores where it is sold in more than one place.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the board, or a single deal at `#/deal/<id>`.
